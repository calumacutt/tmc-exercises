Cleaning up the Movement Library

Taxonomy v3 · proposal · {n_ex} exercises

Four things you asked for, and one idea that ties three of them together: the sheet is trying to record two different things in one column.

Discipline and Line answer “what movement is this?” Games and Prehab & Rehab answer a different question — “what job does this do in a class?” That is why they never fitted. A game of soccer is ball skills that happens to be played as a game; a banded facepull is scapular control that happens to be prehab.

So both dissolve into real movement disciplines, and the class-role information moves to a new tag column. That is your points 3 and 4 — the same fix applied twice. It also retires the Games pillar, taking you back to five.

What changed, and why

Split the lines doing several jobs at once. Horizontal Pull held 29 exercises doing four different things: rows, arm curls, the front lever family, and straight-arm rotation. It comes down to 7. Vertical Pull mixed strict pulling with swinging and kipping, so swinging gets its own line.

Dropped the lines that overlapped each other. Inverted & Rotational Locomotion › Rolls duplicated Ground Locomotion › Rolling Locomotion. Bridges were spread across three disciplines. Straight Arm Balance held a single exercise and its name collided with the Straight-Arm Strength discipline. Rotational / Kick Power overlapped Kicks.

Renamed the lines named after the wrong thing. Prehab & Rehab › Banded was named after a band, which is exactly why the four dumbbell versions of exercises already sitting in it had nowhere to go — equipment is not a category of movement. Likewise Hip Opening (frontal) and (sagittal) become Middle Split Line and Front Split Line: the same distinction, in words a member would recognise.

One rename is worth doing for a purely technical reason. Rhythm, Flow & Expression contains a comma, and multi-value fields are comma- or semicolon-separated. That single comma makes it impossible to reference any of its five lines from Also Appears In — which is why Bridge Circle's reference is broken today. Renaming it Rhythm & Flow removes the problem at the source.

The one thing to check before anything else

Hip Opening (frontal) currently holds 33 exercises that are almost all sagittal — front splits, long lunges, couch stretch, forward folds, Thomas stretch. Meanwhile the pancake / middle splits / frog / butterfly cluster you have been entering has no Line at all, and Hip Opening (sagittal) sits empty. The line's name and its contents disagree. I have swapped them, which re-homes about a sixth of the Mobility pillar. It is the biggest single judgement here and the one most worth your physical read.

09Where I am least confident

The frontal/sagittal swap. Anatomically I believe it is right, but it is a big move and you know the material better than I do.

Partner & Connection as a discipline. You asked for games to go under the discipline that applies to them. For roughly twenty of them — grappling, king of the mat, trust games, partner carries — no movement discipline applies, because partner contact is the content. So I made it a real discipline rather than force them somewhere false. If you would rather retire the handful with almost no movement in them (Name Game, Speed Dating, Paper Scissors Rock, Marry Me), that works too.

I deliberately left one declared line out. Rotational Articulation was declared and empty, and across all {n_ex} exercises I found nothing to put in it. That reads as a gap in the training rather than the taxonomy — there is no standalone rotational articulation work in the library. Declaring an empty line would have hidden that.

Four kettlebell exercises — halo, around-the-world, wood chop, ATW-to-lunge — exposed that core work existed only as a hanging line inside a straight-arm discipline. I gave core its own discipline rather than wedge loaded rotation into a mobility line.
